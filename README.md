# Unnati Portal (उन्नति पोर्टल)

A web platform that connects local village problems with engineering college students, corporate funding, and state government monitoring.

Live Prototype: https://unnati-portal.vercel.app

---

## What is this project?

In rural areas across Jharkhand, people face daily issues with drinking water, farming cold storage, electricity, and health centers. 

Unnati Portal turns these ground-level problems into real-world college projects:
1. Citizens & Village Heads (Panchayats) report problems using text, voice notes, or photos.
2. Engineering Students & Teachers take up these problems as final-year capstone projects.
3. Companies (CSR) fund the prototypes and testing.
4. State Government tracks everything on a live district map.

---

## The 4 Main Roles

- Citizen / Panchayat: Report local issues in regional languages with auto-location.
- Student & Faculty: Pick assigned challenges, build prototypes, and earn academic credits.
- Corporate CSR: Fund student projects milestone-by-milestone.
- State Admin: View district problem heatmaps and college rankings.

---
